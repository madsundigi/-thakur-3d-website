## 4 · FAQ (SP-10 — 8 Q&As, mirrored in FAQPage markup)

1. **When can a puppy have its first bath or groom?** — From about 8 weeks, ideally 10–12 weeks once the first vaccinations have started — very young puppies can't regulate body temperature well. Before that, spot-clean with a damp cloth. Check your pup's vaccination dates on our vaccination page first.
2. **How much does puppy grooming cost?** — ₹699 flat for any breed from 8 weeks to 6 months — the Puppy Intro Groom. After 6 months your pup moves to regular dog grooming from ₹599. There's no travel charge in Ludhiana, and you pay by UPI or cash after the groom.
3. **My puppy isn't fully vaccinated — is a home groom safe?** — Safer than a salon: your puppy never meets other dogs or shared tubs. Every visit uses a fresh, sealed kit opened in front of you, and your background-verified groomer sanitises hands and wears a fresh apron before touching your pup.
4. **How long does it take, and what if my puppy gets scared?** — About 45 minutes, with as many breaks as your puppy needs. The goal is a happy first experience, not a perfect haircut — if your pup gets overwhelmed, we slow down, play, and finish the rest next time.
5. **Will you cut my puppy's hair?** — Usually just a tidy around the eyes, paws and hygiene area. Full haircuts wait until your pup is comfortable with clippers — we introduce the clipper sound and feel gently in the first session.
6. **What should I do before the groomer arrives?** — Let your puppy play and toilet first so they're relaxed, keep a few small treats handy, and have a bathroom or balcony with a tap and a plug point ready. We bring everything else.
7. **Puppy ki pehli grooming kab karwayein?** — 8 hafte ke baad, behtar 10–12 hafte jab pehli vaccines shuru ho jaayein. Pehli grooming chhoti aur pyaar se hoti hai — ₹699 flat, ghar pe. Vaccination ki dates humare vaccination page pe dekhein.
8. **Can vaccination and grooming happen on the same day?** — Ask your vet first — many vets advise waiting a day or two after a vaccine before a bath. Book both on WhatsApp and we'll plan the Puppy Intro Groom around your vaccination dates.

In-answer links: #1, #7, #8 → `/ludhiana/dog-vaccination/` · #3 → `/safety-hygiene/`.
