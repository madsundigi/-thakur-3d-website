## 4 · FAQ (SP-10 — 8 Q&As, mirrored in FAQPage markup)

1. **How much does tick treatment cost?** — ₹399 as an add-on with any groom, or ₹699 as a standalone visit with an anti-tick bath. Both include a full-body tick check and safe removal of visible ticks. Prices are fixed, there's no travel charge in Ludhiana, and you pay by UPI or cash after the service.
2. **When is tick season in Punjab?** — Ticks pick up from April as it heats up, and the monsoon (July–September) is peak season; tick-fever cases rise from May to September. Start prevention in April, check your dog every night during the monsoon, and keep monthly checks through winter.
3. **What are the signs of tick fever?** — Watch for lethargy, loss of appetite, fever, pale gums and dark or reddish urine. Tick fever can be serious if ignored — if you notice these signs, book a ₹699 vet home visit the same day instead of a grooming treatment.
4. **Will one treatment get rid of ticks completely?** — It clears the ticks on your dog that day and protects for a while, but ticks also hide in bedding, wall cracks and gardens. We show you a nightly 60-second tick check and how to wash bedding; heavy infestations usually need repeat treatments and home cleaning, and your vet can advise on longer-lasting prevention.
5. **Can you treat my cat for fleas?** — Yes, with cat-specific products only — many dog anti-tick products contain permethrin, which is toxic to cats, so we never use them on cats. Same prices: ₹399 with a groom or ₹699 standalone.
6. **Is the treatment safe for puppies?** — Many anti-tick products have minimum age and weight limits, so we check your puppy's age and weight first and use only what's labelled safe for them. For very young puppies the vet may recommend a different approach — we'll say so rather than take a risk.
7. **How do I remove a tick myself safely?** — Use a tick tool or fine tweezers: grip the tick close to the skin and pull straight out with steady pressure — no twisting or crushing. Never use kerosene, petrol, burning or petroleum jelly; they can make things worse. Clean the spot, wash your hands, and watch your dog for tick-fever signs.
8. **Dog ko tick ho gaye hain, kya karein?** — Ghabrayein nahi, aur kerosene ya jalaake ticks mat hatayein. Hum ghar aakar anti-tick bath dete hain (₹699, ya groom ke saath ₹399 add-on) aur poore body ka tick check karte hain. Agar dog sust hai ya khana nahi kha raha, toh ₹699 vet visit book karein.
