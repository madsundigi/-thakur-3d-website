## 3. Area-combination patterns (service × locality) — do they deserve pages?

## 4. Hinglish variants — honest assessment

Direct searches for written Hinglish ("kutte ki grooming", "kutta nahlana", "dog nahlane", "pet groomer chahiye", "doggy grooming karwana") returned **zero India-indexed ranking pages** — no competitor targets these in writing, and there is no SERP evidence of dedicated written-Hinglish demand pages. Hinglish queries are real but arrive mostly via **voice search and conversational long-tail**, which Google maps onto the English pages anyway (BERT-era matching). 

**Recommendation: do NOT build pages for Hinglish keywords. Embed them naturally in FAQ copy and image alt text** so voice queries match, e.g.:

| Hinglish pattern (plausible, voice/conversational) | Where to embed | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| "dog ko ghar pe nehlana hai" / "dog nahlana" | Bathing FAQ: "Dog ko ghar pe nehlana ho? Hum aate hain." | nahlana/nehlana = bathing; matches voice phrasing of core service. |
| "dog grooming karwani hai" / "grooming karwana" | Booking CTA microcopy | karwana = get-it-done transactional construction, standard Indian service-query verb. |
| "dog groomer chahiye ludhiana" | FAQ heading variant | chahiye = "need/want" — classic Indian demand phrasing (same pattern as "maid chahiye", "driver chahiye"). |
| "kutte ka doctor" / "dog ka doctor near me" | Vet page FAQ: "Ghar pe dog ka doctor bulana ho…" | "X ka doctor" is a well-known Indian query family for vets/pediatricians. |
| "dog ko tick ho gaye hain kya karein" | Tick blog intro | Symptom-phrased conversational query. |
| "ghar pe dog walking wala chahiye" / "dog walker ka rate" | Walking page FAQ | "wala/wale" agent-noun pattern + "ka rate" price pattern are ubiquitous Hinglish constructions. |
| "dog vaccination ghar pe" | Vaccination page FAQ | Direct code-mix of confirmed English head term. |

Punjabi-script (Gurmukhi) targeting: **skip for Phase 1** — no SERP evidence of Gurmukhi pet-service queries; affluent Ludhiana pet parents search in English/Hinglish.

## 9. Methodology & honesty note

- **No paid keyword tools** (Ahrefs/SEMrush/Keyword Planner) were used. All competition labels are judgments from **who currently ranks** in live SERPs (thin aggregator template = LOW/MED beatable; national brand hub or map-pack-only = MED/HIGH), gathered 2026-10-02 via ~20 web searches across service × modifier combinations and competitor-domain-restricted queries.
- Search volumes are **directional**: Ludhiana-specific long-tails are low-volume individually; the strategy wins on aggregate coverage + map pack + conversion rate, not single-keyword volume.
- Competitor sites thepetnest.com, mrnmrspet.com, petgroomly.com, urbanpetsgrooming.in could not be fetched directly (network egress blocked); their titles, slugs, prices and coverage were mined from SERP snippets and domain-restricted searches — package prices quoted (e.g. MrnMrsPet ₹999/1499/1999/2499) are as shown in SERP data and should be re-verified before publishing comparison content.
- Hinglish variants are linguistic-pattern judgments (no SERP evidence of written-Hinglish ranking pages exists); treat them as voice-search/FAQ seasoning, not page targets.
- Before writing each page, re-check live SERP for its primary keyword — thin-aggregator city pages churn frequently.
