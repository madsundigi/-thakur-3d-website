# Local SEO Playbook 2025–26 — PetDoorStep (Doorstep / Service-Area Business, Ludhiana → Punjab → India)

Research date: 2025-10-02. Scope: Google Business Profile (GBP) for a service-area business (SAB) with no storefront, review engine, citations for India, locality-page rules, Ludhiana link building, and an operating routine.

---

## 0. What actually moves local rankings in 2025–26 (context for every decision below)

Whitespark's Local Search Ranking Factors (the industry-standard expert survey) weights the local pack roughly:

| Signal group | Weight | What it means for PetDoorStep |
|---|---|---|
| GBP signals (primary category, keywords in business name, completeness, activity) | ~32% | The GBP profile IS the product. Primary category is the single biggest controllable factor. |
| Review signals (recency, velocity, rating, keywords in reviews, owner replies) | ~20% and rising; review *recency* is now a top-5 factor | Build a permanent review engine, not a one-time push. |
| On-page signals (title tags, locality content, service pages) | ~19% | The Astro site's service + locality pages carry this. |
| Link signals (local links, topical links) | ~15% | Ludhiana-specific links beat generic directories. |
| Behavioral (clicks, calls, direction requests, bookings) | rest | WhatsApp taps, calls and website clicks from the profile feed back into rank. |
| Citations/NAP | ~7–9% | Foundational, not a growth lever: inconsistent NAP *caps* everything else. Build once, audit quarterly. |

Proximity still dominates (Google estimates ~half of ranking decisions) and is uncontrollable — which is exactly why an SAB in Ludhiana must win every controllable signal, and why locality pages matter for the non-map organic results.

## 1. GBP Setup Checklist — Service-Area Business (step-by-step)

PetDoorStep is a textbook SAB: it travels to customers and serves nobody at its base. Google's rules for this case are strict and most pet-startup suspensions come from breaking them.

### 1.1 Pre-setup decisions (do these before touching Google)

1. **Fix the canonical NAP** and write it in a master doc (use everywhere, character-for-character):
   - Name: `PetDoorStep` — exactly the legal/brand name. **Do NOT keyword-stuff** ("PetDoorStep – Dog Grooming at Home Ludhiana" violates the Business Name guideline and is the #1 re-suspension trigger). The brand name alone, every time.
   - Phone: one dedicated Indian mobile number that is also the WhatsApp Business number (e.g., +91-XXXXX-XXXXX). Never change it casually; it is the join key across every citation.
   - Address: the real operating base (home office/registered office in Ludhiana). It is required for verification but will be **hidden**.
   - Website: `https://petdoorstep.in` (or final domain) — root URL on GBP, not a UTM-less deep link. Add UTM tracking: `?utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=profile`.
2. **Create the Google account** on a business Gmail/Workspace account the founders control (not an agency's).
3. Prepare proof assets for verification: signage/branded T-shirts, branded grooming kit bags, vehicle/scooter branding if any, GST/Udyam registration or utility bill matching the address, business cards.

### 1.2 Profile creation (order matters)

1. Go to `google.com/business` → "Add your business".
2. Business name: `PetDoorStep` (exact).
3. Primary category: **Pet groomer** (see §2 for full category plan).
4. At "Do you want to add a location customers can visit?" → **answer NO.** This is the SAB fork. Google's 2025–26 flow explicitly asks the business model (storefront / travels to customers / SAB without storefront) — pick "I deliver goods and services to my customers."
5. Enter **service areas**: add up to 20 areas. Start with: `Ludhiana`, then the priority localities Google accepts as areas (Sarabha Nagar, BRS Nagar, Model Town, Civil Lines, Dugri, Pakhowal Road, South City, Ferozepur Road, Haibowal Kalan, Kitchlu Nagar — add the ones Google's picker recognises; otherwise use pin codes 141001, 141002, 141012, 141013, 141010). Rule of thumb from Google: the whole service area should stay within ~2 hours' drive of base — for Phase 1 keep it to Ludhiana city + Mundian/Jamalpur fringe. **Service areas do not boost ranking in those areas; they only describe coverage. Proximity to the (hidden) base still drives map rank.**
6. Enter the real base address when asked (needed for verification), then in Business Information → Location, **clear/remove the address so it does not display**. Hidden address + service areas = compliant SAB. Showing a home address customers can't visit is a guideline violation and a suspension risk.
7. Phone + website: the canonical NAP values.
8. Hours: set actual bookable hours (e.g., Mon–Sun 8:00–20:00). SABs must show the hours they accept/attend customers. Use "More hours" later if vet-at-home hours differ.

### 1.3 Verification (expect video)

- In India in 2025–26, new SAB profiles are routinely pushed to **video verification**: a 30–90s continuous video showing (a) surroundings that prove the location (street, landmark), (b) proof of business operation — branded grooming kit, dryer, table, consumables stock, uniforms, service booking screens, (c) proof of authority to manage (GST certificate, visiting cards, unlocking the stock room).
- Do the recording once, calmly, in daylight. Failed/suspicious verifications cascade into suspensions that take weeks to appeal.
- Never verify with a P.O. box, coworking desk, virtual office, or a friend's shop — Google cross-checks records and these trigger hard suspensions.

### 1.4 Post-verification completion (same week)

Complete 100% of the profile — completeness is a measured ranking input:

1. **Description** (750 chars): lead with services + city + trust props. Draft: "PetDoorStep brings professional pet grooming, dog walking and vet care to your doorstep across Ludhiana — Sarabha Nagar, BRS Nagar, Model Town, Dugri, South City and nearby areas. Background-verified groomers, a sanitised kit for every pet, fixed transparent prices and on-time slots. Services: dog & cat grooming and bathing at home, puppy grooming, tick & flea treatment, dog walking, and vet-at-home visits for vaccination, deworming and check-ups. Book on WhatsApp in under a minute." (No URLs, no phone numbers in the description — policy.)
2. **Services section**: add every service under the matching category with price ranges (Google shows these): Dog bath & brush ₹500–1,500; Full dog grooming ₹800–3,500; Cat grooming ₹800–2,500; Puppy grooming intro ₹500–1,000; Tick & flea treatment ₹600–1,500; Dog walking (monthly) ₹3,000–6,000; Vet home visit ₹500–1,500; Vaccination at home; Deworming at home; Nail clipping; De-matting. Write the 300-char service descriptions with locality + intent words naturally ("at your home in Ludhiana").
3. **Attributes**: Online appointments, LGBTQ-friendly etc. as applicable; "Identifies as women-owned" if true.
4. **Opening date**: set it (prominence signal; also unlocks "Years in business").
5. **Photos (minimum viable set, day one)**: logo (720×720), cover (1024×576), 10+ real photos — groomer kit laid out, groomer in branded tee working on a dog, before/after grooms, vehicle/bag, happy pets, team faces. Geo-relevant, real, no stock. Name files before upload (`dog-grooming-at-home-sarabha-nagar-ludhiana-01.jpg`) — harmless and occasionally helpful.
6. **Booking/appointment link**: set the website's booking page URL (`/book/`) as the Appointment link.
7. **Chat**: add WhatsApp via Edit profile → Contact → Chat / social links: `https://wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX?text=Hi%20PetDoorStep,%20I%20want%20to%20book%20a%20grooming%20slot` — in India this button outperforms the call button for under-40 pet parents.
8. **Q&A seeding** (owner-posted, totally allowed): post and answer the 8–10 real pre-sales questions — "Do you groom cats at home?", "Which areas of Ludhiana do you cover?", "What is the price of full grooming for a Labrador?", "Do groomers bring their own water/electricity needs?", "Are your groomers police/background verified?", "Do you sanitise equipment between pets?", "Can I book for the same day?", "Do you handle aggressive dogs?". Answers = keyword-bearing, honest, 2–4 sentences. Monitor weekly for user questions and spam answers (anyone can answer; wrong answers left standing cost bookings).
9. **UTM-tagged links everywhere** so GBP traffic is attributable in analytics.

### 1.5 SAB-specific "don'ts" (suspension traps)

- Don't create a second profile for "PetDoorStep Dugri" etc. — one SAB, one profile per metro until there are genuinely separate staffed branches.
- Don't show the address, ever, while customers can't visit.
- Don't keyword-stuff the name, don't add categories you don't serve (e.g., "Pet store" before Phase 2 supplies actually launch).
- Don't buy reviews, don't review-swap with other local businesses from the same IP/devices.
- Don't list a service area wider than you'll actually serve (entire Punjab now = irrelevance + distrust).

## 2. GBP Category Recommendations

The **primary category is the #1 individual controllable ranking factor.** Google matches queries to categories, not to your description.

| Slot | Category (exact GBP taxonomy name) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | `Pet groomer` | Highest-volume head term ("pet grooming near me", "pet groomer ludhiana"); grooming is the revenue core. |
| Secondary 1 | `Dog walker` | Direct match for the walking service; low competition in Ludhiana. |
| Secondary 2 | `Veterinarian` — **only if a registered vet is formally part of the service**; otherwise use `Pet care service` | "Vet at home ludhiana" queries map here; don't claim it without a vet on the roster (misrepresentation risk). |
| Secondary 3 | `Pet care service` | Catch-all that matches "pet care at home". |
| Secondary 4 | `Dog trainer` (Phase 2 only) | Add when training launches. |
| Secondary 5 | `Pet sitter` (only if offered) | Matches pet-sitting queries. |

Notes:
- Add nothing else at launch. Extra irrelevant categories dilute relevance and invite suspension review.
- Re-check the category list quarterly — Google adds categories (a dedicated "Mobile pet groomer"-type category has appeared in some regions; adopt it as **primary** if/when it shows in India's taxonomy, keeping `Pet groomer` secondary).
- Mirror categories on Bing Places and Apple Business Connect when building those listings.

## 3. GBP Engagement Engine (posts, photos, Q&A — the "alive business" signals)

Behavioral/engagement signals are rising in weight. Cadence that is sustainable beats bursts:

- **Google Posts — 1–2/week.** Rotation: (a) offer post (monsoon tick-package, first-groom ₹200 off — offers are allowed in posts even though incentivised *reviews* are not), (b) before/after transformation with pet's first name + locality ("Bruno from BRS Nagar"— with owner consent), (c) educational tip (tick season in Punjab, double-coat shedding), (d) service spotlight (vet-at-home vaccination drives). Every post: one photo, 100–300 words, one CTA button ("Book", "Learn more") to a UTM-tagged URL.
- **Photos — 3–5/week**, shot by groomers on the job (build it into the job close-out checklist: "2 photos per visit, owner's consent, pet only no faces unless approved"). Mix: results, kit sanitisation shots (trust positioning!), team, localities.
- **Videos** — 1–2/month, <30s: kit sanitisation ritual, a calm cat groom, groomer intro.
- **Q&A** — check weekly; answer new questions within 48h; upvote the correct owner answers.
- **Booking link + WhatsApp chat** kept live; missed-chat rate <5% (Google demotes/disables chat for slow responders).

## 4. Review Engine Design

### 4.1 Policy guardrails (non-negotiable, verified current)

- **No review gating.** Asking only customers who seemed happy, or routing unhappy ones to a private form while happy ones get the Google link, is explicitly prohibited ("discouraging or prohibiting negative reviews, or selectively soliciting positive reviews") and actively enforced — penalties include losing all reviews. Ask **every** customer, same flow.
- **No incentives.** No discount/free add-on/gift for a review, positive or otherwise.
- **Velocity: steady beats spiky.** Google treats sudden spikes as suspect; a steady drip (target: **8–20 reviews/month** at PetDoorStep's booking volumes, i.e., ask after every completed job and expect 15–25% conversion) reads as an active, frequently-chosen business. Review **recency** is now a top-5 ranking factor — an engine, not a campaign.
- Reviews with service + locality keywords in their text ("grooming at home in Model Town") help relevance — you can't ask for keywords, but asking "what service did we do and how did it go?" naturally elicits them.

### 4.2 The ask flow (built into operations)

1. **T+0 (job completion, in person):** groomer's close-out script — "If you were happy with how [pet name]'s groom went, a Google review really helps a small local team like ours. You'll get a WhatsApp link in a few minutes — it takes 30 seconds." Verbal ask + promised link = highest conversion combo.
2. **T+30–60 min (WhatsApp, automated or templated from WhatsApp Business):** send Template A below. WhatsApp is the right channel in India: ~98% open rates vs ~22% for email; measured review-request response rates of ~20%+ vs single digits on email.
3. **T+3 days (one polite nudge, only if no review and no complaint):** Template B. **One** nudge maximum.
4. **Complaint intercepted at any point** → service-recovery flow (call back same day, re-groom free if justified) — this is fine; what's prohibited is making the *review ask itself* conditional on sentiment.

Get the review link from GBP dashboard ("Ask for reviews" / share profile) — short form `https://g.page/r/<code>/review`. Put the same link behind a branded short URL (`petdoorstep.in/review` → 301) so print materials never go stale.

### 4.3 WhatsApp templates

**Template A — post-service ask (EN + Hinglish, pick per customer):**
> Hi {first_name}! 🐾 Thank you for choosing PetDoorStep for {pet_name}'s {service} today in {locality}. We hope {pet_name} is feeling fresh!
> If you have 30 seconds, a Google review would mean the world to our small Ludhiana team: {review_link}
> Aapka feedback hamare liye bahut important hai 🙏 — and it helps other pet parents in {locality} find safe, at-home grooming.

**Template B — single nudge (T+3d):**
> Hi {first_name}, just a gentle reminder from PetDoorStep 🐶 If you were happy with {pet_name}'s {service}, we'd be grateful for a quick Google review: {review_link}. If anything wasn't right, reply here and the founder will personally sort it out.

(That last sentence is an open feedback invitation to everyone — not gating, since the review ask was already made to all.)

**Template C — review thank-you (WhatsApp, after they post):**
> {first_name}, we saw your review — thank you! 🙏 It genuinely helps. {pet_name}'s groom notes are saved; we'll remind you when the next session is due.

### 4.4 QR codes

- Generate a QR pointing at `petdoorstep.in/review` (trackable, re-pointable).
- Deployments: laminated card in every groomer's kit bag (shown at job close), sticker on the kit trolley/bag, thank-you card left behind after each visit, invoice/receipt PDF footer, vaccination-record card given after vet visits, event standees (dog shows, society camps).

### 4.5 Reply strategy and SLAs

Reply to **100% of reviews** — owner responses are an engagement signal, and 88% of consumers prefer businesses that respond to all reviews.

| Review type | SLA | Template skeleton |
|---|---|---|
| 5★ with text | 48h | Thank by name, name the pet + service + locality naturally ("So glad Simba's full groom at home in Dugri went well"), 1 line of personality, invite back. Vary wording — never paste identical replies. |
| 4–5★ no text | 48h | Short warm thanks + one specific detail from the job sheet. |
| 3★ | 24h | Thank + acknowledge the specific gap + what changed + invite to WhatsApp/phone. |
| 1–2★ | **same business day** | Empathise ("sorry this didn't meet the standard we promise"), state the concrete issue back, give the concrete fix (re-groom at no cost / founder callback), move detail offline — never argue publicly, never reveal customer details. After resolution, it's fine to ask them to *consider updating* the review (not demand). |
| Fake/competitor review | 24h flag via GBP "Report review", calm factual public reply meanwhile ("We have no booking under this name…"). | |

Ownership: founder handles ≤3★; ops/marketing handles 4–5★ from a reply playbook doc.

### 4.6 Review schema on the website — current rule (verified)

Since Google's Sept 2019 change — **still in force in current Review Snippet documentation** — pages using `LocalBusiness`/`Organization` markup are **ineligible for star rich results when the reviews are about the business itself** ("self-serving reviews"), whether marked up directly or via an embedded widget. Practical consequences:

- **Do NOT** mark up PetDoorStep's own testimonials with `aggregateRating`/`Review` on LocalBusiness expecting stars — they will not show; it wastes effort and (if fabricated counts) risks a structured-data manual action. It won't tank rankings, but it does nothing.
- **Do** keep testimonials on the site as plain, human-readable content (conversion asset), ideally quoting Google reviews verbatim with first name + locality.
- **Do** still ship `LocalBusiness` (subtype `Petgroomer` doesn't exist; use `LocalBusiness` or `PetStore`? — no: use `LocalBusiness` with `@type: ["LocalBusiness"]`, `name`, `telephone`, `areaServed` listing localities, `url`, `priceRange: "₹₹"`, `openingHoursSpecification`, `sameAs` to GBP/social) **without** self-serving `aggregateRating`.
- Star eligibility survives for **`Product` and `Service` reviews collected by independent third parties** — not worth engineering around; skip it.
- `FAQPage` rich results are now restricted to well-known health/government sites — add FAQ schema if cheap, but expect no visual treatment; the on-page FAQ content itself still matters for AI/answer surfaces.

## 5. NAP Consistency

- One canonical string set (name / full address format / +91 phone / URL) in a shared "NAP master" doc; every listing is copy-pasted from it.
- As an SAB, most directories demand an address: use the real base address consistently, and where the directory allows "hide address"/"service-area" flags, enable them — matching the GBP pattern.
- Citations ≈ 7–9% of rank weight, but **inconsistency caps everything** — Google cross-checks sources and conflicting phone numbers/old addresses erode entity trust.
- Quarterly audit: search the phone number and brand name in quotes; fix drift. When the number or address ever changes, update GBP first, then the top-10 citations within a week.

## 7. Locality Pages Without Doorway Penalties

Google's doorway policy (and the Sept-2025 spam-update enforcement wave) targets near-duplicate city/locality pages where only the place name changes. A locality page is legitimate when it would still be the best page to land on *even if Google didn't exist*. Rules for PetDoorStep's Ludhiana locality pages (Sarabha Nagar, BRS Nagar, Model Town, Civil Lines, Dugri, Pakhowal Road, South City, Ferozepur Road, Haibowal Kalan, Kitchlu Nagar):

**Minimum unique elements per locality page (ship a page only when ALL of these can be filled honestly):**

1. **≥60–70% unique body copy** — not spun; written about the locality (housing mix: kothis vs apartments, typical breeds seen there, parks and walking routes used by the walkers — e.g., Sarabha Nagar's Rose Garden-adjacent lanes vs Dugri phase blocks).
2. **Locality-true logistics block**: actual arrival-window promise for that area, travel-fee rule if any, serviceable pin codes.
3. **Real proof from that locality**: ≥2 reviews/testimonials from customers *in that locality* (quote Google reviews, first name + area), and/or before-after photos geotagged/captioned there. If none exist yet, the page waits.
4. **Locality-specific FAQs** (3–5): parking/society-entry norms ("Do you need gate passes for South City?"), water/power needs in apartments vs kothis, society-specific timing norms.
5. **Distinct data points**: which services are most booked there, slot availability pattern, nearest-vet tie-ups relevant to that area, local landmarks used in directions.
6. **Unique title/H1/meta** beyond city-name swap: "Dog & Cat Grooming at Home in Sarabha Nagar, Ludhiana — same-week slots" vs a different value prop per page.
7. **Structured internal linking**: each locality page links to its 2–3 geographically adjacent locality pages + core service pages; service pages link down to localities — no orphan pages, no 10-link footer blocks of city names stuffed sitewide.
8. **A real conversion path on-page** (embedded booking step-1 + WhatsApp deep link pre-filled with the locality: `wa.me/91…?text=Hi, I want grooming at home in Sarabha Nagar`).
9. **One page per locality per intent** — never multiply (no "dog grooming Sarabha Nagar" AND "pet grooming Sarabha Nagar" pages).
10. **Launch sequencing**: publish 3–4 locality pages at launch (the ones with real anecdotes from pilot customers), then one new page per 2–4 weeks as genuine local proof accumulates. Mass-publishing 10 near-identical pages on day one is the exact doorway fingerprint the 2025 spam update punished.

Safe template architecture: a stable structural skeleton (hero → services+prices → how-it-works → *local proof* → *local FAQs* → booking) is fine; it's the **content layer** that must be local-true. Rough floor: 600–900 words of which the majority is locality-specific.

## 9. Monthly Local-SEO Operating Routine

### Weekly (90–120 min total)
- Mon: publish 1 GBP post (rotation from §3); schedule the week's social mirrors.
- Daily (ops-embedded): photo capture on jobs; upload 3–5 best to GBP weekly.
- Daily: review-ask WhatsApp fires for every completed job (automated); nudges at T+3d.
- Daily: reply to new reviews within SLA (≤3★ same day; others ≤48h); answer GBP Q&A and chat within hours.
- Fri: check GBP for Google-suggested edits ("Google updated your info") and revert wrong ones — SAB profiles get frequent auto-edits; unreviewed changes (e.g., un-hiding address) are a silent killer.

### Monthly (half-day)
1. **GBP Insights pull**: searches (branded vs discovery), top queries, calls/chats/bookings, photo views vs competitors; log in a sheet with month-over-month deltas.
2. **Review audit**: count, average, velocity vs the 8–20/month target, keyword presence; flag/report fakes; refresh templates so replies don't go robotic.
3. **Citations**: build/verify 3–5 next-priority citations (per §6 table) until done; then quarterly NAP drift audit (search phone number + brand in quotes).
4. **Content**: publish 1 locality page **only when its unique-proof checklist (§7) is satisfied**, + 1–2 informational blog posts targeting Ludhiana pet-parent queries; refresh prices on service pages if changed (stale prices kill trust and reviews mention it).
5. **Links**: execute 1 initiative from §8 (one camp, one pitch, one sponsorship per month is enough).
6. **Rank spot-check**: manual incognito + a grid tool if budgeted (LocalFalcon-style scan centred on Sarabha Nagar/Model Town) for "pet grooming at home", "dog groomer near me", "dog walker ludhiana", "vet home visit ludhiana".
7. **Competitor watch**: thePetNest/Mr n Mrs Pet/Petgroomly Ludhiana pages + any new GBP entrants in "pet groomer" Ludhiana; note their review velocity and categories.
8. **Hygiene**: confirm address still hidden, categories unchanged, services/prices current, holiday hours set for upcoming month (Diwali/Gurpurab closures), booking + WhatsApp links working.

### Quarterly
- Full NAP audit across all live citations.
- Category taxonomy re-check (adopt new, better-fitting categories).
- Review the service-area list as coverage expands (prep for Phase-2 cities: replicate this entire playbook per city with a city-specific GBP only when there's a real local base/team there — one GBP cannot rank in Jalandhar/Chandigarh from Ludhiana).
