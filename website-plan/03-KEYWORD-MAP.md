## 1 · SERP landscape summary — who ranks today, and why they're beatable

For every "service + Ludhiana" query, page 1 is thin aggregator city-templates plus directories.
**No competitor has a Ludhiana page with locality-level content, groomer profiles, a transparent
price table, or FAQ schema.** "At home / doorstep" is the modifier every aggregator uses in titles —
PetDoorStep owns it harder (the brand name literally says it).

| Competitor | What ranks | Why beatable |
|---|---|---|
| thePetNest | "Pet Grooming Service at Home in Ludhiana" | Same template as Mumbai/Delhi — zero local content. |

| JustDial | Locality + service category pages (Pet Grooming Services — 92 listings; Dog Grooming Services At Home; Dog Tick & Flea Treatment; Dog Walking) | Domain authority only, zero content depth. Outrankable for mid/long-tail with a real page; categories double as demand proof. |

competition label in §2 cites who ranks so a copywriter can sanity-check before writing.

## 9 · Methodology & honesty note

- **No paid keyword tools** (Ahrefs/SEMrush/Keyword Planner). Every competition label is a judgment
  from **who ranks in live SERPs** (thin aggregator template = LOW/MED beatable; national brand hub
  or map-pack-only = MED/HIGH), gathered 2026-10-02 via ~20 web searches across service × modifier
  combinations and competitor-domain-restricted queries.
- Volumes are **directional**. Ludhiana long-tails are individually low-volume; the strategy wins on
  aggregate coverage + map pack + conversion rate (per `06-CONVERSION-PLAYBOOK.md`), not single-keyword volume.
- Competitor sites (thepetnest.com, mrnmrspet.com, petgroomly.com, urbanpetsgrooming.in) could not be
  fetched directly; all their titles, slugs and prices were **mined from SERP snippets 2026-10;
  re-verify before publishing** any comparison or benchmark claim.
- Hinglish patterns (§5) are linguistic-pattern judgments — no written-Hinglish SERP evidence exists;
  treat strictly as FAQ/alt/voice seasoning.
- **Before writing any page, re-check the live SERP for its primary keyword** (thin aggregator pages
  churn) and log notable shifts in 00 §11 Decision Log. Rank tracking ritual: `09-ANALYTICS-TRACKING.md`.
- PetDoorStep prices cited here are the DRAFT menu from 00 §3.2 — Sunny confirms before launch
  (00 §9 item 5); if §3.2 changes, this file's examples update in the same commit.
