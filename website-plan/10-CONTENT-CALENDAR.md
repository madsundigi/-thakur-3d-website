## 1 · Strategy — why the blog exists

The blog has exactly three jobs. A post that does none of them does not get written.

1. **Win informational keywords** the money pages must not target (research/TOFU–MOFU queries from `03-KEYWORD-MAP.md` §6). The local informational SERP is held by pan-India blogs (Vetic, Supertails, HUFT, VOSD, Virbac) and content farms with **zero Ludhiana coverage** — every post wins by being the only genuinely local, vet-reviewed answer.
2. **Feed internal links to money pages.** Rule of law (from `01-SITEMAP.md` §2 rule 4): *every post links exactly the money page(s) named in its calendar row, with a BOFU anchor in the first half of the post*, plus exactly 2 related posts from its cluster. The calendar table below is the single authority on which post supports which money page.
3. **Build E-E-A-T** for a new domain: named author, health posts carry *"Reviewed by [FILL:VET_PARTNER_NAME], BVSc, Reg. No. [FILL:VET_REG_NO]"*, visible publish + last-reviewed dates, honest scope statements ("when a home visit is NOT enough"), and no claim beyond `00-MASTER-PLAN.md` §3.4.

## 3 · Production SOP — every post, every week

A post is "done" only when all 10 steps pass. Owner: Sunny or the hired copywriter ("Writer"); vet review by [FILL:VET_PARTNER_NAME].

1. **Outline (Mon, ~30 min).** H1 = the working title. First 150 words must contain a 40–55-word direct answer **or** the post's chart/table (featured-snippet position). H2s phrased as the real questions people type (People-Also-Ask style, incl. one Hinglish H2/FAQ where 03 §5 maps one). Mark in the outline where the primary money-page anchor lands (must be in the first half). Plan the post's one table/chart and its 4–6-question closing FAQ.
2. **Draft (Mon–Tue).** Word bands by format — hard band 800–1,500; depth comes from tables and FAQs, not length: guide 1,200–1,500 · price-guide 1,200–1,500 (must contain ≥1 ₹ table; PetDoorStep rows only from `00-MASTER-PLAN.md` §3.2) · listicle 1,000–1,300 · checklist 800–1,100 · seasonal 800–1,100. Voice per `06-CONVERSION-PLAYBOOK.md`. Competitor ₹ figures appear only as context with the † caveat line; health facts must match the vet reviewer's corrections.
3. **E-E-A-T block.** Byline (author name + 1-line bio), and on health posts (vaccination, deworming, tick, vet, skin clusters) the review line: *Reviewed by [FILL:VET_PARTNER_NAME], BVSc, Reg. No. [FILL:VET_REG_NO]* — plus visible "Published" and "Last updated" dates.
4. **SEO pass (Wed morning).** Run the **Quick-20** from `02-SEO-PARAMETERS.md` §4 — all 20 must pass. Title per `03-KEYWORD-MAP.md` §4 blog pattern `{Topic} ({Year} Guide) | PetDoorStep` (drop brand if >60 chars); meta description with the direct answer + one ₹ specific.
5. **Internal links (law — `01-SITEMAP.md` §2 rule 4).** Exactly: the row's primary money page with a BOFU anchor in the first half (e.g. *"book a dog groomer at home in Ludhiana from ₹599"*), the row's secondary money page once in the body (if listed), and 2 related posts from the same cluster. Descriptive anchors, never "click here". Then add the reverse link: the money page's "Learn more" block links this post.
6. **Images per `08-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` §5.** Minimum: 1 hero (1200×675) + 1 chart/table graphic; WebP, lazy-loaded below the fold, alt text containing the keyword naturally (Hinglish alt allowed per 03 §5).
7. **Schema per `04-TECHNICAL-SEO.md`.** `Article` + `FAQPage` (the closing FAQ block) on every post; price tables as real HTML tables.
8. **Publish Wed 10:00 IST** at the row's exact `/blog/<slug>/` URL. Front-matter per §4 below. Tick the week's row in this file's calendar (strike or `[x]` note) and, for Weeks 1–2, tick the Wave-2 tracker line in `00-MASTER-PLAN.md` §6.
9. **Index + track (Wed).** GSC URL Inspection → Request Indexing; verify indexed within 7 days, else re-submit and check internal links reach the post. Add the primary keyword to the rank tracker per `09-ANALYTICS-TRACKING.md`.
10. **Repurpose within 72 h (Thu–Fri).** Per post: **3 Instagram posts** — (a) carousel: the post's table/chart split into 5–7 slides, (b) 20–30 s reel script whose hook is the post's snippet answer, (c) single tip-tile with one ₹ price or threshold rule — and **1 WhatsApp status** (1080×1920 tile: one tip + one ₹ price + "Book on WhatsApp" CTA). All captions point to the post or its money page.

**Lead magnets** (built once, delivered via WhatsApp opt-in per `07-BOOKING-SPEC.md`): W2 printable vaccination tracker · W26 monsoon checklist PDF · backlog R4 new-puppy checklist.
