## Tick season in Ludhiana: what changes for your dog

The tick on almost every Punjab dog is the brown dog tick, and two things about it shape the year. It loves warmth and humidity, so infestations build through summer and peak in the monsoon. And unlike most ticks, it can live and breed entirely indoors — in cracks in the floor, skirting, bedding and sofa seams where your dog rests. That is why a dog that rarely leaves a flat in Sarabha Nagar or Dugri still picks them up, and why the warm months are a question of degree, not of on-or-off.

Three things rise together as the weather warms:

- **Numbers.** More ticks in the environment means more climbing onto your dog, and a dense coat on a Labrador, Golden Retriever or German Shepherd hides them well.
- **Indoor load.** Because the tick breeds inside, a few on the dog in April can become a houseful by July if nothing is done.
- **Disease risk.** More bites means a higher chance of tick fever (babesiosis and ehrlichiosis), which the tick passes into the blood.

If you are already pulling ticks off, a home [tick & flea treatment in Ludhiana](/ludhiana/tick-flea-treatment/) clears the dog and buys you time to get the house and bedding sorted too.

## The nightly 60-second tick check

The best prevention costs nothing: a quick, hands-on check that catches ticks before they attach and swell. Do it after the evening walk, when a tick has usually climbed on but not yet dug in. Work through the spots ticks hide in:

1. **Ears** — inside and under both flaps, a favourite hiding place.
2. **Under the collar** — lift it off and feel the skin underneath.
3. **Between the toes** and around the paw pads.
4. **Armpits and groin** — warm, folded skin where a tick settles unseen.
5. **Face, neck and tail base** — around the eyes, and where the collar sits.

Run your hands over the whole coat feeling for small bumps, then part the fur on anything you find. A torch helps on a dark or thick coat. In peak season make this nightly; in the cool months once a week is enough.

## When five ticks means an infestation

Finding one or two ticks in peak season is normal — remove them and keep checking. But if you are pulling **five or more off in a single day**, or finding them night after night, treat it as an infestation. At that point the ticks you see are a fraction of the ones breeding in the bedding and floor cracks, so the dog and the home both need treating.

One safety point that matters in a dog-and-cat home: anti-tick dosing is specific to the species and the pet's weight, so it should be vet-guided. In particular, **cats cannot process permethrin**, a common ingredient in dog spot-ons, and a dog product put on a cat — or even a cat grooming a freshly treated dog or sharing its bed — can be fatal. Never use a dog's tick product on a cat, and keep them apart for a day or two after a dog is treated.

To remove an attached tick, use fine-tipped tweezers, grip it as close to the skin as you can and pull straight out. Do not burn it, crush it, or dab it with kerosene or petroleum jelly — the old home fixes make the tick push more into the bite and raise the infection risk.

## Warning signs — when to call a vet

Most tick bites heal with nothing more than removal. But tick fever can show up **one to three weeks after a bite — even after you have taken the tick off** — so watch for these signs and book a [home vet visit](/ludhiana/vet-at-home/) or read more on [tick fever in dogs](/blog/tick-fever-dogs-symptoms-treatment/) if they appear:

- Lethargy and a dog that has gone off its food.
- Fever, or shivering that is not about the cold.
- **Pale gums, dark or reddish urine, nosebleeds or pinpoint bruising** — these point to anaemia or low platelets and mean a same-day vet, not a wait-and-see.
- Limping, stiffness or swollen joints that move around.

Tick fever is treatable with antibiotics when it is caught early, which is the whole reason the nightly check and quick removal are worth the minute. If any of the serious signs above show up, confirm with your vet straight away. ## Frequently asked questions

**When is tick season in Punjab?**
Ticks are active year-round in Punjab because the brown dog tick survives indoors, but numbers peak from April to September — building in the April–June heat and worst in the July–September monsoon. January and February are quietest, though a warm, damp spell can wake them up out of season.

**How often should I check my dog for ticks?**
Every night during the April-to-September peak, and once a week in the cooler months. A 60-second check of the ears, under the collar, between the toes, and the armpits and groin catches most ticks before they attach, which is also your best defence against tick fever.

**How many ticks count as an infestation?**
As a rough rule, five or more in a single day, or finding them night after night, is an infestation rather than the odd bite. The ones on the dog are only part of it — the rest are breeding in bedding and floor cracks, so treat the home alongside the dog.

**Are tick products the same for cats and dogs?**
No. Dosing depends on the species and the pet's weight and should be vet-guided. Cats cannot process permethrin, which is common in dog spot-ons, so a dog product used on a cat can be fatal. Keep the two apart for a day or two after a dog is treated, and never share products.
