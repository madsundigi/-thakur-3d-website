## The full first-year maths, visit by visit

A puppy is not one injection; it is a short series. Here is the shape of the first year, so you can count the service fees in advance even before the vaccine MRPs are added.

1. **6 to 8 weeks** — first combined (9-in-1 / DHPPiL) shot. One visit.
2. **10 to 12 weeks** — second combined booster. One visit.
3. **14 to 16 weeks** — final combined booster, usually with the first anti-rabies. One visit.
4. **Every year after** — one combined booster and one rabies shot, same month each year. One visit.

So a puppy's first-year programme is roughly **three to four home visits** — that is ₹199 each in service fees, plus each vaccine at MRP. The exact ages and brand belong to your vet; the full age-by-age chart is in our [puppy vaccination schedule for India](/blog/puppy-vaccination-schedule-india/). ## What changes the price — and what never should

**What legitimately changes it:** which shot is due, and the brand. A single-disease vaccine, a combined 9-in-1 and an imported brand can all sit at different MRPs, and that is set by the manufacturer, not by us. The number of visits a puppy still needs also changes the running total.

**What never should:** your locality, the day of the week, or the size of your dog. A Pomeranian in Sarabha Nagar and a Labrador in Dugri pay the same ₹199 service fee. There is no travel charge inside our Ludhiana service areas, no festival-day surge, and no quote-on-arrival on the vaccine — you see the printed MRP on the vial.

## Are home vaccines genuine? The cold-chain question

This is the real worry behind the price question, and it deserves a straight answer: yes, a home vaccine is as genuine and as effective as a clinic's — on two conditions.

- A **registered veterinarian** gives the injection.
- The vaccine stays in an **unbroken cold chain** (2–8 °C) from the pharmacy to your door.

A vaccine that has been left warm can simply fail, which is why the carried cold box matters more than the walls around the vet. In Punjab's 42 °C May heat, that cold box is not optional. Ask how the vaccine was transported, and ask to see the vial's label and printed MRP before it is drawn up — a good provider will show you without being prompted. That is also your check that the MRP on the bill is the real one.

## How to avoid hidden charges

1. **Separate the two numbers.** Ask for the service fee and the vaccine MRP as two line items, not one bundled figure.
2. **See the vial.** Check the printed MRP on the label before the shot is drawn up.
3. **Confirm there is no travel fee** (there is none inside our service areas).
4. **Keep the record.** A dated vaccination card and a reminder for next year's booster should come as standard.
