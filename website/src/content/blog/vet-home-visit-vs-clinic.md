## The short answer: home or clinic?

Most of the time the choice is obvious once you sort the situation into "routine and stable" versus "urgent or needs equipment". This table does that split.

| Situation | Better choice |
|---|---|
| Routine check-up, vaccination, deworming | Home visit |
| Anxious, senior or multi-pet household | Home visit |
| Minor skin, ear or coat problem | Home visit |
| Trauma, deep wound, heavy bleeding | Clinic |
| Suspected parvo, breathing trouble, collapse | Clinic |
| X-ray, scan, surgery or hospitalisation | Clinic |

If your case sits in the top half, a home vet is the calmer and usually the cheaper route. If it sits in the bottom half, the time you save by going straight to a clinic matters more than the comfort of home.

## What a home vet visit is best for

For preventive and low-risk care, a home visit is often the better medicine. The pet stays in its own surroundings, there is no stressful car ride, and the vet sees how your dog or cat actually behaves at home — not frozen with fear on a steel table.

A [home vet visit in Ludhiana](/ludhiana/vet-at-home/) covers most of what a healthy pet needs through the year:

- **Vaccinations** — puppy courses and annual boosters, given at your door (our [puppy vaccination schedule](/blog/puppy-vaccination-schedule-india/) lays out the full timeline).
- **Routine deworming** — timed by age and weight; the [dog deworming schedule](/blog/dog-deworming-schedule-india/) explains how often.
- **General check-ups** — weight, coat, teeth, ears and a listen to the heart.
- **Minor, stable problems** — a mild skin itch, an ear that needs cleaning, a nail issue, loose motions that are not severe.
- **Senior and anxious pets** — dogs that panic in waiting rooms, cats that fight the carrier, or elderly pets for whom a trip is simply hard.

For a Labrador that shakes the whole way to a clinic, or a Persian cat that hides for a day after one, the home visit is not a luxury — it is the option that gets the care actually done.

## What a clinic does better

A clinic wins the moment a case needs machines, hands-on monitoring or sterile surgery. No home visit — ours included — can carry a full radiology room or an operating theatre to your gate, and it is more honest to say so than to pretend otherwise.

Go to a clinic when the situation needs any of these: an **X-ray or ultrasound**, **blood tests run on-site**, an **IV drip over hours**, **oxygen support**, or **surgery under anaesthesia**. Fracture setting, deep-wound stitching, a stuck labour, or a pet that needs to be admitted and watched overnight all belong in a clinic too. A good home vet will tell you this on the spot and point you to the right place rather than attempt something the setting cannot support.

## When a home visit is not enough — go to a clinic

Some situations are emergencies, and the kindest thing you can do is not wait for a home slot. Head straight to a clinic for any of these.

| Emergency | Why a clinic |
|---|---|
| Road accident, fall, heavy bleeding | Needs X-ray, drip, maybe surgery |
| Suspected parvo in a puppy | Isolation plus IV care and monitoring |
| Breathing trouble, collapse, seizures | Oxygen and watching on site |
| Broken bone or deep wound | X-ray and stitching under anaesthesia |
| Difficult labour that is stuck | May need an emergency C-section |
| Needs a scan or on-site blood test | Equipment only a clinic has |

One more honesty note on timing: our vets visit through the day, not around the clock. For a true middle-of-the-night emergency, do not wait for morning — go to the nearest 24-hour clinic straight away. A home visit is for planned and daytime care, not a replacement for emergency cover.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is a vet home visit as good as going to a clinic?**
For routine and preventive care, yes. A registered vet can vaccinate, deworm, examine and treat common, stable problems at your door. The real difference is equipment: anything needing an X-ray, a scan, IV fluids or surgery still belongs in a clinic, and a good home vet will tell you so.

**How much does a vet home visit cost in Ludhiana?**
A home consult is a flat ₹699, whether the vet comes to Sarabha Nagar, BRS Nagar or Dugri, with no travel charge in our service areas. Any medicines or vaccines are billed separately at their printed MRP — the same price as a pharmacy.

**Ghar pe dog ka doctor bulana ho — kya yeh safe hai?**
Haan. A registered veterinarian visits, examines your pet in its own calm surroundings, and tells you honestly if the case actually needs a clinic. For routine care at home it is both safe and far less stressful than a waiting room.

**When should I take my dog to a clinic instead?**
For any emergency — a road accident, heavy bleeding, suspected parvo, trouble breathing, collapse, a seizure, or anything likely to need an X-ray or surgery. For a middle-of-the-night emergency, skip the home booking and go straight to a 24-hour clinic.

**Can vaccinations and deworming be done at home?**
Yes. Both are routine, low-risk and ideal for a home visit. Puppy courses, annual boosters and scheduled deworming are exactly the kind of care a vet can give calmly at your door, with a reminder for the next due date.
