Diwali pet care in Ludhiana has to begin before the first cracker goes off, not on the night itself. The hard part for a frightened pet is not one evening — it is a six-week run of noise. In 2026 it builds from Dussehra in late October, peaks on Diwali (8 November) and the nights either side, flares again at Gurpurab in late November, and ends with New Year's Eve on 31 December. If your dog or cat bolts, shakes or stops eating when crackers start, that fear is something you can plan around: walk before dusk, keep one quiet room ready, and know when a daytime walk slot or a calming vet visit is worth booking.

## Punjab's cracker season is not one night

National pet blogs treat Diwali as a single evening. In Ludhiana it is a long run, and a dog that was fine last year can still struggle with the sheer number of nights. Here is roughly how 2026 lays out, so you can prepare for the whole stretch instead of being caught out each time.

|---|---|---|
| Dussehra | Late October | Evening bursts; noise building up |
| Diwali | Around 8 November | Heaviest; loud nightly for a week |

By Gurpurab the Ludhiana evenings have turned cold and foggy, so the safe-room routine below also keeps a thin-coated dog like a Pomeranian or a young Indie warm while it waits the noise out.

## Walk before dusk: the daytime plan

Tire your pet out in daylight and the evening is far easier. Ludhiana dusk in November is around 5:30 pm, and crackers ramp up soon after dark, so aim to finish the main walk by about 5 pm. ## Build one safe room

The single most useful thing you can do is prepare one quiet room before the noise starts, and take your pet there early each evening.

1. Pick an interior room away from the street — a bathroom, a store room or a bedroom on the quieter side.
2. Shut the windows and draw the curtains. This muffles the bangs and blocks the sudden flashes.
3. Leave a radio, TV or fan running for steady background sound that softens the cracks.
4. Put down familiar bedding and an unwashed t-shirt of yours — your scent is reassuring.
5. Keep water and a favourite chew or toy in the room, and for a cat, the litter tray and a box to hide in.
6. Make sure the collar and ID tag are on, in case a gate is left open for guests and your pet bolts.
7. Stay with them if you can, and act normally. Do not punish the fear, and do not drag a hiding pet out to face it.

## Warning signs — when to call a vet

Most pets ride out cracker season shaken but fine. Call a vet, though, if your pet refuses food or water for more than a day, hurts itself trying to escape (torn nails, cuts, bleeding paws), trembles or pants for hours after the noise has stopped, or swallows a spent cracker. If there is a burn or heavy smoke exposure, cool the area with plain water and get to a vet — do not apply ointments or home remedies first. When in doubt, a quick call to your vet is the safest move.

## Frequently asked questions

**Mera kutta patakhon se darta hai — what should I do on cracker nights?**
Bring him inside before dusk and settle him in one quiet room with the curtains closed and a radio on. Stay calm yourself, because dogs read your mood, and never scold the fear. If it is severe every year, speak to a vet before the season, not on the night.

**When is Diwali 2026, and how long does cracker season last in Ludhiana?**
Diwali falls on 8 November 2026, but the noise is not limited to that night. It builds from Dussehra in late October, peaks across Diwali week, returns for Gurpurab in late November, and ends on New Year's Eve, so plan for the whole stretch.

**Should I still walk my dog during Diwali?**
Yes — but in daylight, finished before dusk. A proper daytime walk tires your dog so it settles more easily at night. Skip evening walks while crackers are going off, both for the noise and the smoke on the roads.

**Can I give my dog a sedative for the crackers?**
Only one a vet has prescribed for your own dog after seeing it. Human medicines and borrowed pills can be dangerous at the wrong dose. A vet can suggest a calming plan and tell you whether medication is even needed.

**My cat hides for the whole festival — is that a problem?**
Hiding is normal for cats and usually the healthiest thing they can do. Give it a quiet room with a box to hide in, its litter tray and water, and leave it be. If it stops eating or drinking for more than a day, call a vet.
