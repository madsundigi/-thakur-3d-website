## What a monthly dog-walking price should include

A low monthly figure means nothing if the service behind it is thin. Before you compare prices across cities, make sure you are comparing the same thing. A fair plan, anywhere in India, should include:

- A **fixed walker** — the same person each day, not a rotating pool of strangers.
- A **photo and GPS update** after every walk, so you know it happened and your dog is fine.
- A **background and reference check** on the walker, done before they ever hold your leash.
- A **meet-and-greet** with your dog before the first paid walk.
- **Weather sense** — in Ludhiana that means moving walks around the 44°C April–June afternoons, not marching a Labrador out at 2 p.m.

If a quote is cheaper because it drops the updates, the fixed walker or the heat rules, it is not cheaper — it is a different, riskier service.

## How to vet a dog walker before you book

Price is the easy part; trust is the hard part, because you are handing your family member to someone while you are not there. Run any walker — ours or anyone else's — through this checklist before the first month:

1. **Background and ID check** — ask plainly whether the walker is ID- and reference-verified. A real service says yes without hesitating.
2. **A fixed walker, not a rotating pool** — your dog should see the same face every day and build a bond.
3. **A photo or GPS update after every walk** — proof the walk happened, where it went, and that your dog came back fine.
4. **A meet-and-greet first** — the walker should meet your dog on a leash before day one, so the first real walk is not with a stranger.
5. **Hot-weather rules** — in Ludhiana's April–June heat, walks must move to before 8 a.m. and after 7 p.m., with a 5-second hand test on the tarmac before any paw touches it.
6. **A written plan, no advance** — the monthly price and schedule confirmed in writing on WhatsApp, and payment only after walks are done.
