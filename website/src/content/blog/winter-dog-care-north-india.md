Winter care for dogs in north India comes down to three changes once Ludhiana slips below about **15°C** in late November: move the walks, protect the skin and coat, and keep thin-coated and senior dogs warm. From **mid-December to late January**, our mornings sit at **4–7°C** under dense fog and smog — the worst time of day to take a dog out — so the single biggest change is to walk later in the day. Below is a month-by-month plan for a Punjab winter: when a sweater genuinely helps, how to stop the dry, flaky skin that peaks in January, and the warning signs that mean a vet visit.

## Winter in Ludhiana: what changes for your dog

Ludhiana's winter runs from November to late January, and it is not a gentle one. By December the city wakes under thick fog, the air-quality index climbs as crop-burning smoke and traffic smog settle low, and night temperatures in Dugri or BRS Nagar drop to 4–7°C. Three things about that weather matter for your dog:

- **The air is worst at dawn.** Fog and smog pool overnight and lift only once the sun gets going. A 6 am walk means both the coldest air and the dirtiest, which is hard on puppies, flat-faced breeds like Pugs, and any dog with a heart or breathing condition.
- **Floors turn cold.** The marble and tile most Ludhiana homes are built on act like a cold slab all day. Dogs that sleep on the floor lose heat through it, and older joints stiffen fastest there.
- **The air dries out.** Low humidity plus indoor heaters strips moisture from skin and coat, so dandruff, flaking and itchy patches peak in January.

If the coat is matted or shedding heavily going into winter, it is worth booking a [dog grooming session at home in Ludhiana](/ludhiana/dog-grooming/) before the cold sets in — a clean, de-shed coat insulates far better than a matted one.

## Do dogs need a sweater in a Punjab winter?

Some do, many don't. The rule of thumb: below about 15°C a sweater or coat helps dogs that cannot make their own warmth — and does nothing useful for dogs that already carry a thick double coat.

**Likely needs a sweater:**

- Thin, single-coated or small breeds — Pugs, Beagles, Dachshunds, Dobermans, Greyhounds and many small Indies.
- Puppies under about four months, who cannot regulate heat well yet.
- Senior dogs and any dog that is unwell, underweight or recovering.

**Usually does not:**

- Double-coated breeds built for cold — Labradors, German Shepherds, Golden Retrievers, Pomeranians, Huskies and Spitz.

Watch the dog, not just the thermometer: shivering, a tucked tail, lifting paws off cold ground or refusing to settle all mean it is cold. For a double-coated breed, never shave the coat down for winter — that undercoat is the insulation.

## Your dog's winter daily routine

A simple daily rhythm covers most of what a north-Indian winter demands:

1. **Walk late, not at dawn.** Aim for 11 am to 3 pm on fog-and-smog days, and keep walks a little shorter when it is bitterly cold.
2. **Dry the paws and belly** after every walk — damp fur in cold air chills a dog fast, and winter grit cracks paw pads.
3. **Layer up the thin-coated ones** with a well-fitted sweater before they step out below 15°C.
4. **Raise the bed off the floor** and add a blanket, especially for seniors on marble.
5. **Keep water fresh and food steady** — dogs still need to drink in winter even when they ask for less.
6. **Brush daily** to keep the coat lofted and the skin oils moving.

## Warning signs — when to call a vet

Most winter care is routine, but a few signs mean it is time for a [home vet visit](/ludhiana/vet-at-home/) rather than another blanket:

- Shivering that does not stop once the dog is warm and dry.
- Limping, stiffness or trouble standing up — cold stiffens arthritic joints, and older dogs feel it most.
- Cracked or bleeding paw pads.
- Flaky skin with hair loss, redness or constant scratching.
- Coughing, laboured breathing or lethargy on high-smog days, especially in flat-faced breeds.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do dogs need sweaters in India?**
Below about 15°C, yes — for thin-coated and small breeds, puppies and senior dogs. Double-coated breeds like Labradors, German Shepherds and Pomeranians make their own warmth and usually do not need one. Watch for shivering or a tucked tail as your real signal, not just the thermometer.

**Thand mein subah dog ko walk karana chahiye?**
On foggy December and January mornings, no — the air is coldest and the smog heaviest at dawn. Walk between 11 am and 3 pm once the sun is up, and keep it shorter when it is very cold.

**Why does my dog get dry, flaky skin in winter?**
Low humidity and indoor heaters dry the skin, and over-bathing makes it worse by stripping natural oils. Bathe less often in winter, brush daily, and use a conditioning groom. If the flaking comes with hair loss or constant scratching, have a vet check it.

**How do I protect my senior dog in a Punjab winter?**
Keep it off cold marble with a raised, well-padded bed, keep walks short and gentle, and watch for stiffness getting into or out of a lie-down. Cold worsens arthritis, so if your older dog is limping or slow to rise, confirm the plan with your vet.
