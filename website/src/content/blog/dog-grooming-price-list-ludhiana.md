## Dog grooming prices in Ludhiana by size

Every package is priced by your dog's size. If you are not sure which band your dog falls in, the examples in the first column will place most dogs.

| Package | Small (under 10 kg) | Medium (10–25 kg) | Large (over 25 kg) |
|---|---|---|---|

| **Full Groom** (most booked) | ₹1,199 | ₹1,499 | ₹1,899 |

**Size examples.** Small — Shih Tzu, Pomeranian, Pug. Medium — Beagle, Cocker Spaniel, Indie. Large — Labrador, German Shepherd, Golden Retriever.

## What's included at each price

The gap between the three packages is what the groomer does, not a better or worse groomer. Every visit is done by the same trained team at your home.

- **Bath & Brush** — bath, blow-dry and a full brush-out, plus a nail trim and ear clean.
- **Full Groom** — everything in Bath & Brush, plus a breed-appropriate haircut and styling and a paw and sanitary trim.
- **Premium Spa Groom** — everything in a Full Groom, plus de-shedding or de-matting, a conditioning masque and a light finishing perfume.

If you mostly need your dog to smell clean and shed less around the house, Bath & Brush is enough. If the coat has grown out or needs shaping, the Full Groom is the one most Ludhiana families book.

## What changes the price — and what never should

**What legitimately changes it:** the size band, and the package. A heavily matted coat can also push a Bath & Brush into de-matting territory, which belongs to the Spa Groom — but the groomer will tell you that **before** any clippers come out, never as a surprise on the bill.

**What never should:** your locality, the day of the week, or "the dog was a bit difficult". There is no travel charge inside our Ludhiana service areas, no festival-day surge, and no quote-on-arrival. The number in the table above is the number you pay.

## How to avoid hidden charges

1. **Ask for the price by size and package** before you confirm — any honest groomer can give it in one line.
2. **Confirm what's included** so a "full groom" is not quietly a bath.
3. **Check for a visit or travel fee** (we charge none inside our service areas).
4. **Get it in writing on WhatsApp**, so the quote and the bill are the same thing.
